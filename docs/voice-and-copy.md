# Comic.me voice

> The lines people read while they wait, celebrate or get stuck. The words live in `src/lib/copy.ts`, so the voice stays consistent.

## The feel
- **Playful, confident, creative, a little mischievous, human, concise.**
- Talk like a sharp friend who happens to run a comic studio, not like software.
- One wink per screen at most. The joke never gets in the way of what to do next.

## Rules
1. **Short.** One idea per line. Cut adjectives first.
2. **Comic words, not AI words.** Write, draw, ink, letter, sketch, panel, cover. Never "generate", "AI-powered", "seamless", "unlock", "leverage", "magic" (we show the magic instead).
3. **Specific beats clever.** "Inking 12 of 40 pictures" beats "Working on it…".
4. **Errors: clear first, charming second.** Say what happened and what to do. A light touch is fine ("Our writer hit a wall"), never a joke about someone's money or photos.
5. **Money and privacy are said plainly.** "Nothing gets drawn (or paid for) until you approve the storyboard." "Never your story text, photos or character sheets."
6. **Rotate, don't repeat.** Celebrations have several lines (`pick()` chooses one per comic), so the same joke doesn't land twice.

## Examples
| Instead of | Say |
| --- | --- |
| Generation completed. | Your questionable life choices are now illustrated. |
| Processing your story… | Reading your story. Twice. |
| Please review the storyboard. | Your comic, before the ink. Change anything: it's all free until you approve. |
| Error: generation failed. | Our writer hit a wall. Try again? |
| Shared to the community. | On the Explore wall. Others can borrow the format, never your story. |
| Welcome! Start by telling us your story. | What's the story? Who's in it, and what happened? |

## Not this
- Meme overload, "bestie", emoji walls, ALL CAPS shouting (comic lettering excepted).
- Being cute where someone is anxious: payments, photos of real people, failures that cost time.
